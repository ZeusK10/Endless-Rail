# Endless Rail V1.3 — Scenic World & Camera Overhaul

## Development checkpoints
The V1.3 work lives on a separate feature branch, not on `main`. Commits are organized by:
1. Collapsible driving panels and non-selectable gameplay HUD.
2. Seeded route geography, shorter biomes, rivers/lakes, hills and terrain-integrated tunnel corridors.
3. Anchored free-look, safer exterior rigs and simplified train interior viewpoints.
4. Three-chunk village settlements, biome wildlife and detailed steel rails/ballast.
5. Calibrated optional derailment and deterministic regression tests.

## Technical invariants
- World features are functions of absolute rail distance and seed, not spawn order.
- Reversing to previously visited terrain reproduces it without requiring save storage.
- No local save, account, backend or multiplayer is introduced.
- Station operations remain a future phase.
- Scene streaming must not retain an unbounded number of chunks; train, fauna and village meshes share prototype geometry.
- Detailed views still depend on the user's performance quality setting.

## Automated verification
The GitHub Actions workflow runs `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, and Chromium Playwright interaction smoke tests. Simulation tests cover route determinism, terrain continuity, river/tunnel generation, camera terrain sweep, repeated world streaming, bounded chunk counts, seeded village/wildlife layouts, and simulated derailment probability.

## Manual acceptance checklist — required before merging for release
- **Desktop Chromium/Firefox/Safari:** drive 5–10 km on several seeds; watch rivers, hillside turns, arched tunnels, bridge crossings and biome changes.
- **Reverse revisit:** drive 2 km, switch direction when stopped, revisit; look for water seams, terrain cracks, discontinuous railbeds, reused geometry and props.
- **All six camera viewpoints:** use cab and carriage interior, then chase, elevated, side, front, and anchored free-look. Try each near hills, in tunnels, over water and during derailment/recovery.
- **UI & inputs:** collapse top and bottom independently; confirm icon-only compact controls, settings slideout, recoverability, safe-area positioning and no text selection while dragging.
- **Sounds:** confirm horn, rail-wheel roll, smoke, water/ambient mix, and speed-dependent sound on speakers/headphones.
- **iPad Safari:** test landscape and portrait, Bluetooth controller and touch, user-gesture audio unlocking, WebGL continuity and thermal/battery behavior over 15 minutes.
- **Performance:** inspect FPS, draw calls, GPU memory, and frame spikes during biome/settlement streaming; adjust adaptive-quality thresholds if required.
- **Optional failures:** with derailment toggled off, no failures. With it on, drive overspeed into bends and verify warning probability, event audio/animation and recovery.

Automated browser tests cannot establish performance, sound authenticity or Bluetooth controller functionality on a physical iPad. Those items remain manual acceptance gates.
