# Audio recordings

The following recordings are bundled locally, so gameplay makes no requests
to third-party audio hosts. All three sources are published by their creators
under **CC0 1.0 / public domain dedication**, permitting modification and
redistribution, including commercial use. Credits here are provided
voluntarily for traceability.

| Game file | Original author and recording | Original source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `public/audio/horn.mp3` | kangaroovindaloo — *Locomotive Horn (whistle)* | https://freesound.org/people/kangaroovindaloo/sounds/278176/ | https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ |
| `public/audio/idle.mp3` | rabban625 — *DieselTrain.wav* | https://freesound.org/people/rabban625/sounds/243052/ | https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ |
| `public/audio/rolling.mp3` | rabban625 — *Train-043.wav* | https://freesound.org/people/rabban625/sounds/436487/ | https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ |

The in-game MP3 files are excerpts transcoded from the creators' publicly
available Freesound previews. Each was downmixed to mono at 22.05 kHz and
compressed to 48–56 kb/s for a small browser download. The game adds dynamic
speed/throttle mixing, generated rail-joint accents, braking and safety effects.

This is a stylized soundscape; the recordings do not directly correspond to
the exact 3D locomotive model. Other procedural sound effects are generated
locally by Web Audio.
